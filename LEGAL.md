# COGBox - legal notice, disclaimer and takedown contact

*Notice version 2 (COGBox 0.5.0). The launcher's "Welcome to COGBox!" screen is a short summary of this page; when this
text changes in a way that matters, the notice version goes up and the launcher shows the summary again.*

## 11. Concerns and takedown requests

If you believe something in the COGBox project infringes your rights, or you have any other legal concern, please
contact us first. We'd much rather fix things quickly and in good faith.

- **Email:** `[CONTACT EMAIL - Shagg to set up, e.g. a dedicated cogbox.legal@... address]`
- **Or:** open a ticket on the project's GitLab page (<https://gitlab.com/shagg88/cogbox>) saying only
  that you have a legal concern and how to reach you. Please don't post private details publicly.

Please include:
1. who you are and, if you act for someone else, who you represent;
2. the work or trademark you believe is affected;
3. exactly what in COGBox you're referring to (file, release, page or line, with links);
4. how to contact you;
5. for a copyright notice under the U.S. DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)): a statement that you have a good-faith belief
   the use isn't authorised, a statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you're
   authorised to act, and your physical or electronic signature.

**What we do:** we confirm receipt, usually within a few days. While we look at the notice, we can take the material in
question down; if it's clearly valid we remove it from current releases and the source and tell you when it's done.
If we believe a notice is a mistake, we'll say why, and where appropriate use the counter-notice process of our host.
Notices sent to our host (GitLab) are handled under the host's own terms and copyright policy.
