Works best when the game already runs at 60 fps or more. Each frame waits for your screen's next refresh, so the picture never tears. With G-Sync / FreeSync the screen follows the game below its refresh rate. The frame rate is capped a little under your screen's refresh rate (half of it with frame generation), which keeps the extra input lag small. Off = frames are shown at once (lowest lag, may tear). = 每一帧都会等待屏幕的下一次刷新，因此画面绝不会撕裂。使用 G-Sync / FreeSync 时，屏幕在低于其刷新率时跟随游戏。帧率被限制在略低于屏幕刷新率的位置（启用帧生成时为其一半），从而让额外的输入延迟保持很小。关：帧会立即显示（延迟最低，可能出现撕裂）。
Sets the graphics boxes in one go. Original = the game as on the console. Change any box afterwards and the preset shows Custom. = 一次性设置所有图形选项。原版：与主机上的游戏一致。之后更改任何选项，预设会显示为自定义。
How much detail the game draws: 1x: 1280x720 as on the console, 2x: 2560x1440, 3x: 3840x2160 (4K), 4x: 5120x2880. Real detail, not an upscale. Match my monitor: the smallest scale that covers the screen, resampled to its exact size. 1080p: drawn at 720p and upscaled to the screen with sharpening - fast, for weaker graphics cards; softer than real detail. = 游戏绘制的细节程度。1x：与主机一致的 1280x720，2x：2560x1440，3x：3840x2160（4K），4x：5120x2880。是真实细节，而非放大。匹配我的显示器：能覆盖屏幕的最小倍率，再重采样到屏幕的精确尺寸。1080p：以 720p 绘制并锐化后放大到屏幕，速度快，适合性能较弱的显卡；比真实细节更柔和。
