Xenia, Xenia Canary and Xenia Edge (BSD 3-Clause)
=================================================

COGBox's renderer (src/layer0_xenos, built into every port program gow1.exe / gow2.exe / gow3.exe / gowj.exe) ports
or adapts code and rules from the Xenia Xbox 360 emulator and its Canary (e6cd2e4) and Edge (5045c19) forks: texture
loading and formats, tiling and mip layout, resolve and gamma rules, the rectangle-list geometry shader, PM4 and
shader-translation rules. The rexglue SDK runtime (rexruntime.dll, rexgpu-xenos.dll) is also derived from Xenia
(licenses\rexglue-sdk\LICENSE). Xenia, Canary and Edge are under the licence below (text of
https://github.com/xenia-project/xenia/blob/master/LICENSE; the forks keep the same LICENSE file).
