Box art

COGBox does not ship any box art. When you press UPDATE ART, it downloads the front box
scans and backgrounds for Gears of War, Gears of War 2, Gears of War 3 and Gears of War:
Judgment from the LaunchBox Games Database (https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com), with x360db
(https://github.com/xenia-manager/x360db) as the fallback.

The scans were contributed to the LaunchBox Games Database by its community. Thanks to
LaunchBox and its contributors. The cover artwork is the property of Microsoft / Xbox Game
Studios and Epic Games. It is downloaded only for your own library view and stays on your PC.
